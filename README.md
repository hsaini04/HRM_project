# Lutron SkillBridge — Employee Training & Skill Development System

An academic MERN prototype prepared for **HRM-203 CA-2 (Q3)**. It shows how the simulated
Gurugram office of Lutron Electronics (India) Pvt Ltd could plan, assign and track training
against a role-based competency framework, with a focus on the **Lighting Design Specialist** role.

> **Demonstration data only — not actual Lutron Electronics HR records.**
> All employees, ratings, assignments and metrics are fictional and generated by a repeatable
> seed script. This is a student project; it is not an official Lutron Electronics system and
> does not use or represent any internal Lutron tool, logo or policy.

---

## Why this system

The CA-1 field study identified three HR challenges for the Gurugram office:

1. **Retention of specialised talent** — experienced lighting-design and system-engineering staff are hard to replace.
2. **Hiring for niche technical roles** — few candidates arrive with lighting-control, CAD/BIM or commissioning skills.
3. **Continuous skill development** *(primary focus of this prototype)* — new products, control systems and tools require ongoing, structured learning.

| CA-1 field-study estimate (indicative, not audited) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Current workforce | ≈ 100–105 employees |
| Near-term requirement | + 15–25 employees |
| Planning gap | ≈ 18–23 employees |

### How the demo office follows CA-1

The demo dataset is sized and shaped by the CA-1 workforce profile, but every person in it is
fictional. It holds **102 employees** (the working figure CA-1 uses for its gap analysis), split by
the mid-points of the CA-1 share ranges, normalised to 100% because the ranges as printed add up to
87–98%. CA-1 groups field service with customer service, so that category covers two departments here.

| CA-1 employee category | CA-1 share (est.) | Demo office |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales & Business Development | 18–20% | 21 (20.6%) |
| Lighting Design / Applications / Engineering | 27–30% | 31 (30.4%) |
| Customer Service / Field / Project Support | 14–16% | 17 (16.7%): 8 system sales & field service, 9 customer service & project support |
| IT / HR / Finance / Administration | 18–20% | 21 (20.6%) |
| Management / Functional Leadership | 10–12% | 12 (11.8%) |

The organisation-wide dashboard shows the same comparison, calculated from the live records.

SkillBridge addresses challenge 3 directly and supports 1 and 2 by making skill gaps, training
progress and career paths visible, so specialists can be developed internally.

## Modules

| Route | Module | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/demo-access` | Demo access | Pick a role: HR/Admin, Manager or Employee (fictional personas, no password). |
| `/dashboard` | Dashboard | Role-specific KPIs, training status, gaps by department, upcoming due dates, activity. |
| `/employees` | Employee management | Search, filter, sort, add, edit and remove fictional employees (HR). |
| `/employees/:id` | Employee profile | Competencies (required vs current), assigned training, development plan, role profile. |
| `/skills` | Competency matrix | Expected level per role (1–4) with current levels of employees in that role; competency library. |
| `/skills/gaps` | Skill gap analysis | Every gap (required − current, never negative), severity, and suggested training. |
| `/training` | Training catalogue | Programmes with delivery mode, hours, level, skills covered and target roles (HR can add/edit/delete). |
| `/assignments` | Training assignments | Assign programmes, change due dates, update progress; status is derived automatically. |
| `/analytics` | Learning analytics | Completion, participation, hours, scores, department and role breakdowns, monthly trend. |
| `/career-paths` | Career development | Illustrative role tracks and individual development plans with goals. |

### Roles (demo context, not authentication)

| Role | Default sign-in ID | Persona (fictional) | Can |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HR / Admin | `DEMO-1003` | Ananya Rao | Everything: employees, competencies, programmes, assignments, plans, analytics. |
| Manager | `DEMO-1001` | Meera Kapoor (Design & Applications Manager) | View the whole office; assess, assign and plan for **direct reports** only. |
| Employee | `DEMO-1007` | Priya Nair (Lighting Design Specialist) | View own profile, gaps, training and plan; update own progress and goal status. |

To sign in, pick the role on the demo access page; each card shows its sign-in ID and no password
is needed. The API creates these three accounts on startup if they are missing (see
[Seed the demo data](#3-seed-the-demo-data)), so the sign-in page works on a fresh database.

The selected role is sent with each request in `X-Demo-Role` / `X-Demo-Employee` headers and
the API enforces the same rules (403 for actions outside the role). This is a demo switch, **not**
a login system — see [Limitations](#limitations).

### Competency framework

- **Categories:** Core, Functional (technical), Behavioural.
- **Proficiency scale:** 1 Awareness · 2 Working · 3 Proficient · 4 Advanced.
- **Gap** = required level − current level, floored at 0 (exceeding the requirement is not a gap).
- **Severity:** gap 1 = Moderate, 2 = High, 3 = Critical; a **priority gap** is 2 or more levels.
- Changing an employee's job role, or a role's requirements, re-syncs their required levels.

### Training status rules

Status is never set by hand; it is derived from progress and the due date:

| Condition | Status |
| --- | --- |
| progress = 100 % | Completed |
| due date before today and progress < 100 % | Overdue |
| progress > 0 % | In Progress |
| otherwise | Not Started |

### Light and dark theme

The interface has a light and a dark theme. It follows the operating system setting until the user
picks one with the sun/moon toggle (in the top bar, and on the demo access page); the choice is then
saved in the browser (`localStorage`) and applied before the first paint, so pages do not flash.
Charts read their grid, axis and label colours from CSS variables, so they switch with the theme.

## Getting started

### Prerequisites

- Node.js **20.19+** (developed on Node 22) and npm
- MongoDB running locally on `mongodb://127.0.0.1:27017`, **or** your own MongoDB Atlas connection string

### 1. Install dependencies

```bash
npm run install:all
```

(Equivalent to `npm --prefix server install` and `npm --prefix client install`.)

### 2. Configure environment variables

Copy the example files and adjust if needed. The real `.env` files are git-ignored — never commit them.

```bash
cp server/.env.example server/.env
cp client/.env.example client/.env
```

| File | Variable | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `server/.env` | `PORT` | `5050` | API port |
| `server/.env` | `MONGODB_URI` | `mongodb://127.0.0.1:27017/lutron_skillbridge` | Database connection (use your own Atlas URI if not local) |
| `server/.env` | `CLIENT_URL` | `http://localhost:5173` | Allowed CORS origin(s), comma-separated |
| `server/.env` | `NODE_ENV` | `development` | Environment name |
| `server/.env` | `AUTO_SEED` | `true` | Create the default demo accounts on startup when they are missing; `false` turns this off |
| `client/.env` | `VITE_API_URL` | *(empty)* | Only needed if the API is on another origin; in development Vite proxies `/api` to port 5050 |

### 3. Seed the demo data

```bash
npm run seed
```

The seed **clears and recreates** the demo collections using a fixed random seed, so every run
produces the same fictional records (dates are relative to the day you run it). Expected output:
33 job roles, 27 competencies, 102 employees (4 not yet assessed), 1,432 competency ratings
(412 gaps, 70 priority), 18 programmes, 253 enrolments (104 completed, 64 in progress, 43 not
started, 42 overdue), 12 career plans and 587 activity entries.

You can skip this step on a new database: when the API starts and the default sign-in accounts are
missing, it seeds an **empty** database with the full demo dataset. If the database already holds
records, it only adds the missing accounts and deletes nothing. Run `npm run seed` whenever you want
a clean reset.

### 4. Run in development

Use two terminals:

```bash
npm run dev:api
```

```bash
npm run dev:client
```

Open <http://localhost:5173>, choose a role on the demo access page, and explore.

### Run as a single app (production build)

```bash
npm run build
npm start
```

The API serves the built client from `client/dist`, so the whole app runs on <http://localhost:5050>.

### Tests

```bash
npm test
```

Runs the Node test runner on the business rules (gap calculation, severity, and enrolment status derivation).

## API summary

Base URL: `/api`. JSON in and out. Errors use `{ "error": { "message", "details" } }` with
status 400 (validation / bad id), 403 (role not allowed), 404 (not found) or 409 (duplicate).

| Method | Endpoint | Roles | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GET | `/health` | all | API and database status |
| GET | `/demo/personas` | all | Demo personas for the role selector |
| GET | `/meta` | all | Departments, roles, levels, delivery modes and other lookups |
| GET | `/dashboard/summary` | all | KPIs, status mix, department gaps, due dates, activity |
| GET | `/dashboard/training-analytics` | all | Analytics datasets (department, role, month, delivery mode) |
| GET | `/dashboard/alerts` | all | Overdue and due-within-7-days assignments in scope |
| GET | `/employees` | all | List with search, department, role, manager, sort, pagination |
| POST | `/employees` | HR | Create employee (required levels synced from the role) |
| GET | `/employees/:id` | all* | Profile with competencies, training and plan |
| PATCH | `/employees/:id` | HR | Update employee |
| DELETE | `/employees/:id` | HR | Remove employee and their learning records (409 while they still manage others) |
| GET | `/competencies` | all | Competency library |
| GET | `/competencies/matrix` | all | Role framework with current levels of employees in the role |
| POST / PATCH | `/competencies`, `/competencies/:id` | HR | Create / update competency and role requirements |
| GET | `/employee-competencies/:employeeId` | all* | Ratings for one employee |
| PUT | `/employee-competencies/:employeeId` | HR, Manager† | Record an assessment (current levels) |
| GET | `/skills/gaps` | all* | Gap rows with severity and suggested training |
| GET | `/trainings` | all | Programmes with search and filters |
| POST / PATCH / DELETE | `/trainings`, `/trainings/:id` | HR | Manage programmes |
| GET | `/enrollments` | all* | Assignments with status, search and sort |
| POST | `/enrollments` | HR, Manager† | Assign one or more programmes |
| PATCH | `/enrollments/:id/progress` | HR, Manager†, own employee | Update progress, score and notes |
| PATCH / DELETE | `/enrollments/:id` | HR, Manager† | Change due date / remove assignment |
| GET | `/career-paths` | all | Illustrative role tracks |
| GET | `/career-plans` | all* | Development plans |
| POST / PATCH | `/career-plans`, `/career-plans/:id` | HR, Manager† | Create / update plans |
| PATCH | `/career-plans/:id/goals/:goalId` | HR, Manager†, own employee | Update a goal's status |

\* Employees only see their own records. † Managers can act on their direct reports only.

## Verification done

- **Automated:** 8 unit tests for gap calculation, severity and status derivation (`npm test`).
- **API:** endpoints exercised with curl, including validation (400), role (403) and duplicate (409)
  errors, progress updates recalculating KPIs, reassessment, and employee create/delete.
- **Browser (manual, Chromium):** every route checked as HR at a 1024 px desktop width; dashboard,
  skill gaps and training assignments also checked as Manager and Employee; dashboard, assignments and
  the navigation drawer checked at a 375 px phone width. Flows exercised: employee search, programme
  create/validate/delete, progress updates (status and KPIs recalculating), persona switching.
- **Theme and contrast (Chromium, 1024 px):** a scripted text-contrast check (WCAG AA: 4.5:1, or 3:1
  for large text) on every route in both themes, with no failures apart from a disabled pagination
  button; every page and the competency-assessment drawer also reviewed visually in the dark theme.
  Chart axis labels checked for clipping and wrapping.

Not done: cross-browser testing beyond Chromium, screen-reader testing, load testing or a security review.

## Limitations

- **No real authentication.** The role selector is a demo convenience; anyone can pick any role.
  A real deployment would need login (hashed passwords or SSO), sessions/tokens and server-side
  identity instead of request headers.
- **Synthetic data only.** Workforce figures, competencies, levels, programmes and career tracks are
  illustrative proposals, not Lutron Electronics' actual framework, policies or records.
- **Single office, single language,** no notifications by email, no file uploads or certificates.
- **No audit trail beyond a simple activity feed,** and no data retention or privacy controls.
- **MongoDB standalone:** multi-document writes are not wrapped in transactions.
